Q: What evaluation metrics are reported in the paper?

TOPK=10

ANSWER:
The evaluation metrics reported in the paper include:

- Accuracy
- Precision
- F1-score
- Classification report
- Confusion matrix
- Graph-based Gradient Boosting Classifier (HistGradientBoosting) metrics
- RandomForest feature importance scores
- LogisticRegression feature importance scores
- SVM features and classification accuracy
- LDA features and classification accuracy

These metrics provide insights into the performance of different models, including their ability to predict trends in music data.
